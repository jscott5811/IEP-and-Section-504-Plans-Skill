# Verified sources

Every factual claim this skill makes about the law must trace to one of the
sources below. All are U.S. federal sources — federal rules apply in all
50 states. Where a detail is set by the state or district instead, the skill
must say so.

## U.S. Department of Education guidance (read directly)

### 1. OCR + OSERS joint resource: Long COVID under Section 504 and the IDEA
- **Link:** https://sites.ed.gov/idea/files/ocr-factsheet-504-20210726.pdf
- **What it is:** Issued jointly July 26, 2021, by ED's Office for Civil
  Rights (OCR) and Office of Special Education and Rehabilitative Services
  (OSERS). Covers both laws side by side.
- **What it establishes:**
  - Section 504 "prohibits disability discrimination and ensures that
    students with disabilities have equal access to educational
    opportunities," applies to schools receiving federal financial
    assistance, and is enforced by OCR.
  - IDEA Part B ensures a free appropriate public education (FAPE) for
    children with disabilities ages 3 through 21; OSERS's Office of Special
    Education Programs (OSEP) administers IDEA.
  - IDEA Child Find: public agencies must identify, locate, and evaluate
    all children with disabilities who need special education and related
    services, regardless of severity.
  - IDEA evaluation: if the agency suspects a disability, it must seek the
    parent's consent; the evaluation must be completed within 60 days of
    consent (or the state-established timeline); a group of qualified
    professionals plus the parent then determines eligibility and the
    child's educational needs.
  - Section 504 eligibility: physical or mental impairment that
    substantially limits a major life activity; the student does **not**
    need to be limited in learning.
  - Section 504 evaluation: must be timely and individualized; may not rest
    on diagnosis alone; a group of people knowledgeable about the child,
    the evaluation data, and placement options (parents, nurses, teachers,
    counselors, psychologists, administrators, social workers, doctors)
    reviews the results and decides placement and supports. Example
    support: additional time to finish classwork and tests.
  - Section 504 reevaluations: required periodically and before any
    significant change in placement.

### 2. OCR: Parent and Educator Resource Guide to Section 504 in Public Elementary and Secondary Schools (Dec 2016)
- **Link:** https://www2.ed.gov/about/offices/list/ocr/docs/504-resourceguide-201612.pdf
- **What it is:** OCR's main parent-facing guide to Section 504 (cited in
  ED's own enforcement and guidance documents).
- **What it establishes:** How Section 504's FAPE duty works in K-12
  schools — regular or special education and related aids and services
  that meet the student's needs as adequately as the needs of students
  without disabilities are met.

## Federal regulations (IDEA, 34 CFR Part 300)

- **34 CFR §300.8** — defines "child with a disability" (the IDEA
  disability categories).
- **34 CFR §300.39** — defines specially designed instruction.
- **34 CFR §300.111** — Child Find duty.
- **34 CFR §300.300** — parental consent required before initial
  evaluation and before initial provision of services.
  (Reg text: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2008-title34-vol2/pdf/CFR-2008-title34-vol2-sec300-300.pdf)
- **34 CFR §300.301(b)–(c)** — a parent may request an initial evaluation;
  it must be conducted within 60 days of receiving parental consent (or the
  state timeline).
  (Reg text: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2015-title34-vol2/pdf/CFR-2015-title34-vol2-sec300-301.pdf)
- **34 CFR §300.320(a)(2)** — every IEP must include a statement of
  measurable annual goals, including academic and functional goals,
  designed to meet the child's disability-related needs and enable
  progress in the general education curriculum.
- **34 CFR §300.503** — prior written notice: if the district refuses a
  parent's evaluation request, it must explain why in writing.

## Additional federal source

- **Congressional Research Service, "The Individuals with Disabilities

  — summarizes the required IEP contents (present levels, measurable
  annual goals, services, progress reporting).
  https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R41833

## What these sources do NOT cover

- State-set evaluation timelines and state 504 procedures — verify with
  the state education agency.
- Legal advice about any individual child's eligibility — point to the
  district's procedural safeguards notice and the state's Parent Training
  and Information Center.
